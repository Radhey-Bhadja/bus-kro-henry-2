TV Series Timeline

From 2006 to 2019 — five seasons of horrid adventures on CITV and Nicktoons.

2006 — 2007

Season 1

The chaos begins! 52 episodes introducing Henry, Peter, Margaret, and the whole gang. Premiered on CITV.

52 Episodes
2008 — 2009

Season 2

Another 52 episodes. Henry's pranks get bigger, the Secret Club gets stronger, and the Purple Hand Gang fights back!

52 Episodes
2010 — 2011

Season 3

52 more episodes! Aired alongside the 2011 live-action movie "Horrid Henry: The Movie" in 3D.

52 Episodes
2014 — 2015

Season 4

Another 52 episodes bringing the total to 208. Henry becomes more of a scapegoat while Margaret gets more devious.

52 Episodes
2018 — 2019

Season 5

Final 42 episodes on Nicktoons UK. The series became the #3 most-watched animation in Britain!

42 Episodes

From the original Horrid Henry (1994) to Horrid Henry: Up, Up and Away (2019), follow Henry's most horrid moments in short, hilarious chapters perfect for reluctant readers.
